About Us

A Royal Dosa Experience

Mr. Crown Fancy Dosa began with a simple dream — to give every dosa lover a fancy, flavoursome and unforgettable experience. Today we craft over a dozen varieties, each made fresh to order on our traditional griddles with premium ingredients.

  • Fresh to Order — every dosa is made only when you order it.
  • Signature Chutneys — coconut, tomato and fiery red chutneys made in-house.
  • Royal Ambience — dine in style at our crown-themed outlet.
  • Friendly Pricing — fancy taste without burning a hole in your pocket.
